The Sinaloa and Jalisco New Generation Cartels (CJNG), \ntogether with their procurement, distribution, and financial support networks stretching across \nLatin America, China, and other key global nodes, remain the dominant threats for the trafficking \nof these and other drugs into the United States. The cartels capitalize on the relative ease of synthetic drug \nproduction compared to the physical and environmental limitations of traditional plant-based drug \nproduction to generate immense revenues. The cartels maintain steady supply chains for obtaining \nthe precursor chemicals, primarily from China and India, necessary to produce these synthetic \ndrugs.
